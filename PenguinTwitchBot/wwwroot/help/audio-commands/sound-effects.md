# Audio Commands & Sound Effects

The [Audio Commands](/actions/audiocommands) dashboard allows viewers and streamers to trigger audio clips and sound effects on stream via chat commands.

## Configuring Audio Commands

- **Command Name**: The chat trigger (e.g. `!airhorn`, `!applause`, `!bruh`).
- **Audio File**: Select an audio file from your local sound effects library.
- **Cooldowns**: Set minimum and maximum cooldowns to maintain a clean listening experience.
- **Rank Permissions**: Restrict loud or sensitive audio commands to VIPs, Subscribers, or Moderators.
- **Point Costs**: Require viewers to spend channel points or tickets to trigger premium sounds.

## Sub-Actions & Triggers

- Audio commands can also be triggered directly within Actions using the `PlaySound` sub-action.
- Audio playback can route to desktop audio or dedicated virtual cables for precise OBS audio mixer management.
