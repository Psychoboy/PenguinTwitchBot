### WeatherAPI Setup

Enables the `!weather` chat command. Viewers can check current weather conditions and daily forecasts for any location or view the stream's default location.

#### Obtaining a WeatherAPI Key
1. Sign up for a free account at [WeatherAPI.com](https://www.weatherapi.com/signup.aspx).
2. Verify your email address and log in to your API Dashboard.
3. Copy your API key from the dashboard.
4. Paste the key into the Weather tab, enable the toggle, and click **Test Connection**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Supported Location Formats:**
> Chatters can query weather using US ZIP codes (`90210`), UK postcodes (`SW1`), city names (`London`, `Seattle, WA`), or coordinates. Set your default location in **Bot Settings &rarr; General**.
