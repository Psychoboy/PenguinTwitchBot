# Winner Privacy & Contact Info

To protect winner personal information on-screen during live streams, sensitive columns are masked by default.

## Masked Contact Columns

- **Email & Claim Information**: Viewer email addresses submitted during claiming are obscured with placeholder dots (`••••••••`).
- **Show / Hide Toggle**: Click the eye icon in the table header to unmask email addresses when you need to contact winners privately off-stream.
- **Data Protection**: Sensitive contact data is kept secure in your local database and is only exposed to authorized broadcaster and editor accounts.
