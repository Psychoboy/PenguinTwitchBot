# Giveaway Winners History

The [Giveaway History](/giveaway/history) view logs all historical giveaway winners across your broadcasts.

## Historical Records

The table records:
- **Winner Username**: Twitch handle of the selected winner.
- **Prize**: Name/description of the awarded item.
- **Winning Date**: Timestamp when the drawing took place.
- **Prize Tier**: Classification tier assigned to the prize.
- **Claimed By**: The moderator or admin account who approved and delivered the prize.
- **Inline Editing**: Streamers can update or correct prize details and claimed status directly in the table rows.
