# Known Bots Overview

The [Known Bots](/community/known-bots) manager allows streamers and moderators to register known bot accounts that frequent Twitch channels (e.g. `Nightbot`, `StreamElements`, `Moobot`, `Lurxx`, `SoundAlerts`, `CommanderRoot`).

## Managing Known Bots

- **Add Known Bot**: Enter the Twitch username in the **Add Known Bot** card on the left and click **Add Bot**.
- **Remove Known Bot**: Click the Delete icon next to any bot in the table to remove it from the exclusion list.
- **Search & Filter**: Search through registered bots using the quick search filter bar.
- **Self & Bot Account**: The broadcaster account and the bot account itself are always automatically recognized and protected across all systems.
