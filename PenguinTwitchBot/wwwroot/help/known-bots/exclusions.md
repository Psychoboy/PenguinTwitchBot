# Automated Bot Exclusions

Registering known bots safeguards your channel statistics, economy, and giveaways from being distorted by automated bot activity.

## Where Known Bots Are Excluded

1. **Viewer Chat & Active Time Metrics**:
   - Known bots are excluded from active chatter lists and watch time accrual calculations.
2. **Point Accrual & AddActive Rewards**:
   - Bots will not receive points during automatic interval payouts (`AddActive`) or bulk manual commands (`!addactivepoints`).
3. **Giveaway Raffles & Draws**:
   - Known bots cannot accidentally win giveaways, tickets, or random viewer drawings (`SelectRandomViewers` sub-action).
4. **Auto Shoutouts**:
   - Automated chat bots are prevented from triggering auto-shoutout messages or clip playback.
5. **Interactive Minigames**:
   - Bots cannot participate in community games such as FFA, Heist, Gamble, or Defuse.
