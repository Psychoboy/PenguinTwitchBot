# User-Assigned Voices

Personalize your stream by giving viewers, subscribers, and VIPs their own distinct voices whenever their chat or TTS redemptions are voiced aloud.

---

### Assigning a Voice to a Viewer

1. Open **Voices** (`/voices`).
2. Under the **Active & User Voices** tab, view existing viewer mappings.
3. In the right-hand panel:
   - Enter the Twitch **Username**.
   - Select the **Voice Engine** (Kokoro, Piper, Google).
   - Select the specific **Voice Name** from the dropdown.
   - Click **Assign Voice**.

---

### Managing User Voices

- **Filter & Search**: Use the search box above the user voices table to find specific viewers by name.
- **Audition**: Click the play icon on a viewer's row to preview what their assigned voice sounds like.
- **Delete / Reset**: Click the delete icon to remove a custom voice assignment. The viewer will automatically revert to the system fallback voice on their next TTS message.
