# Themes & Emote Customization

The **Themes & Seasons** tab gives full visual control over how Parachute Drop and Plinko Bounce look on your stream.

## Built-In Seasonal Presets

The bot ships with six curated seasonal & holiday presets:
- **Spring**: Pastel pink cherry blossom canopies, gentle floral tones, sakura petal puffs, and meadow green targets.
- **Summer**: Sunny golden sands, sky blue ocean canopies, refreshing swimming pool target basin, and water droplets.
- **Fall**: Autumn amber and maple leaves, harvest orange chutes, deep multi-tier crunch leaf piles, and acorn droplets.
- **Winter**: Crisp snowdrifts, snowy winter canopies with ice blue platforms, and soft blizzard puffs.
- **Halloween**: Spooky pumpkin orange & witch purple tones, flapping bats, bubbling toxic green cauldron, candy corn bursts, and witch hats.
- **Christmas**: Festive Santa red, evergreen pine garland, star sparkles, holiday gift box target with red ribbon bows, and Santa hats.

## Custom Themes

Create unlimited custom themes with your own visual assets:
- **Backgrounds**: Transparent or custom backdrop images.
- **Canopies & Platforms**: Custom parachute domes and target platforms.
- **Peg Art**: Custom pin textures for Plinko.
- **Colors**: Primary, secondary, highlight, and board border accent colors.

## Emote & Image Support

You can assign multiple images or Twitch emotes for characters, droplets, and tokens:
- **Multiple Random Selection**: Add multiple emotes or URLs (e.g. `sptvShots, sptvHype, sptvLUL`). The bot randomly picks from the pool for each viewer or droplet spawn.
- **Twitch Native & Third-Party Emotes**: Type standard emote codes (e.g. `sptvShots`, `LUL`, `PogChamp`, BTTV, 7TV, FFZ). The bot resolves them automatically.
- **Full Animated Support**: Animated Twitch emotes and GIFs/WebPs animate at a full 60 FPS in both the web preview card and live on the OBS canvas overlays.
- **Media Library & Uploads**: Upload local images or pick from your Bot Media Library directly from the picker.
