# Themes & Emote Customization

The **Themes & Seasons** tab gives full visual and audio control over how Parachute Drop, Plinko Bounce, and Balloon Ascent look and sound on your stream.

## Built-In Seasonal Presets

The bot ships with curated seasonal & holiday presets ready out of the box:
- **Spring**: Pastel pink cherry blossom canopies, gentle floral tones, sakura petal puffs, and meadow green targets.
- **Summer**: Sunny golden sands, sky blue ocean canopies, refreshing swimming pool target basin, water droplets, and sunny balloons.
- **Fall**: Autumn amber and maple leaves, harvest orange chutes, deep multi-tier crunch leaf piles, acorn droplets, and rustic hot air balloons.
- **Winter**: Crisp snowdrifts, snowy winter canopies with ice blue platforms, frosted cloud targets, and soft blizzard puffs.
- **Halloween**: Spooky pumpkin orange & witch purple tones, flapping bats, bubbling toxic green cauldron, candy corn bursts, witch hats, and jack-o'-lantern balloons.
- **Christmas**: Festive Santa red, evergreen pine garland, star sparkles, holiday gift box target with red ribbon bows, Santa hats, and ornament balloons.

## Custom Themes & Graphic Assets

Create unlimited custom themes with your own visual assets:
- **Backgrounds**: Transparent overlays or custom backdrop illustrations.
- **Canopies & Platforms**: Custom parachute domes, balloon canopies, and target landing platforms/clouds.
- **Plinko Peg Art**: Custom pin textures and bucket styling.
- **Colors**: Primary, secondary, highlight, and board border accent colors.
- **Custom Sound Bundles**: Assign dedicated audio stings for round starts, round ends, launches, wins, bullseyes, pops, and collisions from your Bot Media Library.

## Emote & Image Support

You can assign multiple images or Twitch emotes for characters, hazards, droplets, and tokens:
- **Multiple Random Selection**: Add comma-separated emotes or URLs (e.g. `sptvShots, sptvHype, sptvLUL`). The bot randomly picks from the pool for each viewer or hazard spawn.
- **Twitch Native & Third-Party Emotes**: Type standard emote codes (e.g. `sptvShots`, `LUL`, `PogChamp`, BTTV, 7TV, FFZ). The bot resolves them automatically.
- **Full Animated Support**: Animated Twitch emotes, GIFs, and animated WebPs animate at a full 60 FPS in both the web management preview and live on OBS canvas overlays.
- **Media Library & Uploads**: Upload local files or pick directly from your Bot Media Library with one click.
