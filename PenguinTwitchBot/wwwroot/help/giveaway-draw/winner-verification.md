# Winner Display & Past Winners

When a winner is drawn, PenguinTwitchBot displays their details prominently on the dashboard and in chat.

---

## Winner Information Card

The winner celebration card provides immediate broadcaster verification:
- **Twitch Username**: Identifies the drawn entrant.
- **Prize & Tier**: Confirms the specific prize and prize tier awarded.
- **Following Status**: Displays whether the drawn user follows your Twitch channel (`Following: True` or `False`).

---

## Redrawing & Multiple Winners

- **Redraw / Next Winner**: If a drawn winner is absent, ineligible, or if your giveaway awards multiple prizes, click **Draw Winner** again (or run `!draw` in chat) to select the next winner.
- **Past Winners Log**: The collapsible **Past Winners** panel at the bottom of the page archives all winners drawn during the session along with prize records.
