# Viewer Moderation Actions

Directly from the viewer details view, moderators and broadcasters can perform essential management and moderation tasks.

## Available Actions

- **Giveaway Exclusions**: Disqualify or ban suspicious users or alts from participating in future giveaways with a single click.
- **Point Adjustments**: Correct point balances or wipe farmed currency using point management buttons or default chat commands (`!addpoints`, `!removepoints`, `!setpoints`).
- **Channel Moderation**:
  - Initiate chat timeouts or permanent bans.
  - Review recent chat message history from the viewer to investigate reported behavior.
- **Automation & Sub-Actions**:
  - Use `CheckPoints` and `GiftPoints` sub-actions to automate custom reward or penalty actions for specific users.
  - Target viewers dynamically in actions using the `{User}` or `{UserId}` placeholders.
