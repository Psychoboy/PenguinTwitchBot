# Fish & Shop Management

The Fishing Game Manager gives broadcasters complete control over the fish species, shop inventory, game settings, and player data in the channel's fishing mini-game.

---

## Fish Types Catalog

Under the **Fish Types** tab:
- **Species Parameters**: Configure species name, rarity tier, base weight (kg), base gold payout, and category tags (e.g., *Freshwater*, *Deep Sea*, *Reef*).
- **Rarity Tiers**: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Mythic. Rarity determines baseline encounter likelihood and gold scaling.
- **Images**: Assign custom artwork for each species. Uploaded images are automatically processed into optimized WebP formats.
- **Active Toggle**: Enable or disable specific fish types to rotate seasonal catches or retire event-exclusive species.

---

## Equipment & Shop Items

Under the **Shop Items** tab, define gear that viewers can purchase with their earned fishing gold:
- **Equipment Slots**: Rod, Reel, Line, Lure, Bait, Hook, Tackle Box, Net, and Special items. Viewers can equip one item per slot (plus permanent passive bonuses).
- **Boost Types**:
  - *General Rarity Boost*: Increases odds of encountering higher rarity tiers (Uncommon+).
  - *Star Boost*: Improves the chance of catching 2-star and 3-star trophy quality fish.
  - *Weight Boost*: Increases the caught fish's physical weight, maximizing leaderboard scores.
  - *Specific Fish / Category Boost*: Targets specific species or families (e.g., Sharks or Salmon).
- **Consumables vs. Permanent Gear**: Items can be permanent equipment (unlimited uses) or consumables with limited uses (such as bait).
- **Generate Defaults**: Click *Generate Default Items* to populate a pre-balanced progression roster of starter rods, lines, and lures.

---

## Player & Economy Management

- **Settings Tab**: Adjust global catch probabilities, boost mode multipliers, and stamina/energy settings.
- **Player Management Tab**: View individual player profiles, gold balances, equipped loadouts, and catch histories. Admins can grant or deduct gold and items directly.
