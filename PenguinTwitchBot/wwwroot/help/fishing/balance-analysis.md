# Fishing Balance Analysis

The Fishing Balance Analysis dashboard provides data-driven telemetry into player activity, economy flow, and catch distributions across your broadcast history.

---

## Analytics & Date Filtering

- **Date Range Filters**: Set a custom Start Date and End Date, or leave them empty to analyze all-time historical fishing data.
- **Run Analysis**: Aggregates all recorded catches, shop transactions, and player progression statistics for the selected interval.

---

## Key Metrics & Economy Health

- **Catch Volume & Velocity**: Total casts made, successful catches, and active participating viewers.
- **Rarity Breakdown**: Compares observed catch frequencies against mathematical base probabilities to detect unintended anomalies.
- **Gold Faucets & Sinks**: Tracks total gold generated from selling fish versus total gold spent on gear upgrades and consumables in the shop.
- **Top Performing Species**: Highlights the most profitable fish species caught across your community.

---

## Data Export

- **JSON Export**: Export raw telemetry data for custom scripts or external reporting tools.
- **CSV Export**: Export formatted tabular datasets ready for import into Excel or Google Sheets.
