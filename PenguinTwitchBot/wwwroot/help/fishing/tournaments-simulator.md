# Tournaments & Simulator

Engage your community with competitive fishing tournaments and test your game economy using the built-in simulator.

---

## Tournament Management

Under the **Tournaments** tab:
- **Creation & Scheduling**: Name the tournament, set start and end dates/times, and establish entry fees (in viewer points or fishing gold).
- **Eligibility & Scoring**:
  - *Open Scoring*: Any caught fish counts towards the tournament score.
  - *Target Species / Category*: Restrict scoring to specific catches (e.g., heaviest Tuna, most Bass caught, or Deep Sea creatures only).
  - *Scoring Criteria*: Rank participants by single heaviest catch, total cumulative weight, or total catch count.
- **Reward Brackets**: Define 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place prizes using loyalty points, flat fishing gold bonuses, or percentage cuts of the pooled entry fees.
- **Active & Archive Lists**: View live standings in real-time or review past tournament podiums.

---

## Catch Simulator

The **Simulator** tab allows streamers and editors to model thousands of virtual casts in seconds:
- **Simulation Parameters**: Specify the number of casts (from 10 to 10,000+), hypothetical player gear loadouts, and active boost modes.
- **Rarity & Distribution Curve**: Observe the resulting percentage breakdown between Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Mythic catches.
- **Economy Projections**: Verify average gold payout per cast and projected inflation before releasing newly created fish or shop items to your live audience.
