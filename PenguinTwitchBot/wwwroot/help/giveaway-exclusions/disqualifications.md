# Giveaway Exclusions & Disqualifications

The [Giveaway Exclusions](/giveaway/exclusions) view manages accounts explicitly disqualified from entering channel giveaways.

## Managing Exclusions

- **Adding Exclusions**: Enter the Twitch username, an optional expiration date, and reason in the right-hand form.
- **Permanent vs Temporary**: If no expiration date is provided, the exclusion remains indefinitely. For temporary disqualifications (e.g. cooldown after winning a major prize), set an expiration date after which the viewer can enter giveaways again automatically.
- **Audit Reasons**: Store notes explaining why the user was disqualified (e.g. "Won major prize 30-day cooldown", "Suspected giveaway alt account").
- **Automatic Bot Safety**: Accounts registered in [Known Bots](/community/known-bots) are excluded by default without needing manual entry here.
