# Banned Songs Management

Prevent inappropriate, troll, or copyrighted tracks from ever being requested or played on stream.

---

### Banning a Song

1. **Accepted Input Formats**:
   - Standard URLs: `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ`
   - Short URLs: `https://youtu.be/dQw4w9WgXcQ`
   - YouTube Shorts: `https://www.youtube.com/shorts/dQw4w9WgXcQ`
   - Embed URLs: `https://www.youtube.com/embed/dQw4w9WgXcQ`
   - Live stream URLs: `https://www.youtube.com/live/dQw4w9WgXcQ`
   - Raw 11-character Video ID: `dQw4w9WgXcQ`
2. **Metadata**:
   - Provide an optional friendly **Title** to easily identify the track.
   - Provide an optional **Reason** (e.g., "Copyright", "Troll", "DMCA", "Profanity").
3. Click **Ban** to save the entry.

---

### Managing Banned Tracks

- **Search**: Use the search box above the table to quickly filter by title, video ID, or reason.
- **Unban**: Click the trash can icon next to any entry to remove the ban, allowing the track to be requested again.
- **Direct Link**: Click the Video ID link to verify the video on YouTube in a new tab.
