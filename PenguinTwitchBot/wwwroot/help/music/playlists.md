# Playlist Management

Create and curate collections of YouTube music for your stream's background audio.

---

### Managing Playlists

- **Create a Blank Playlist**: Click **New Playlist** to start a fresh collection. Provide a unique name and start adding YouTube tracks.
- **View & Edit Songs**: Click the eye icon (**View**) on any playlist to inspect all tracks, preview videos, edit titles, and remove songs.
- **Deleting Playlists**: Click the trash icon to delete a playlist. Note: You cannot delete the playlist currently designated as your default playlist.
- **Refresh Unplayed Status**: Click the refresh icon (**Refresh Unplayed Songs**) to reset all tracks across your active playlists back to unplayed status so the full pool can be randomized again.

---

### Adding & "Stealing" Songs

- **Adding Tracks**: Inside the playlist view, enter any valid YouTube URL or 11-character video ID to add tracks to that specific playlist.
- **"Stolen" Songs (`!steal` / `!stealsong`)**:
  - Whenever a viewer requests a great track on stream, you can save it with one click by hitting the **Steal** button on the Music Player, or typing `!steal` / `!stealsong` in chat.
  - **Important**: Any stolen song is automatically saved directly into your **Default Playlist**, making it part of your regular background rotation forever without having to manually look up the URL.
