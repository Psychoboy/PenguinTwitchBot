# Song Request Cooldowns

Prevent repetitive song requests and promote musical variety during your broadcast.

---

### Global Cooldown Settings

- **Enable Song Request Cooldowns**: Turns on automatic cooldown tracking across all song requests.
- **Cooldown Duration (Minutes)**: How long a requested song remains on cooldown before it can be requested again (e.g. 60 to 180 minutes).
- **Exempt Skipped / Vetoed Songs**:
  - When enabled, if you or a moderator skip or veto a song (`!skip`, `!veto`), its cooldown is immediately cleared so viewers can request it again later without waiting out the timer.

---

### Manual Song Cooldowns

You can manually place specific songs on cooldown without waiting for them to be requested:
1. Enter the YouTube URL or raw Video ID.
2. Enter an optional title to identify the song.
3. Specify the cooldown duration in minutes.
4. Click **Add Cooldown**.

---

### Managing Active Cooldowns

- The **Active Song Cooldowns** table displays all tracks currently locked by cooldown, showing remaining time and expiration timestamps.
- **Clear Single Cooldown**: Click the trash can icon next to a song to remove its cooldown immediately.
- **Clear All Cooldowns**: Click **Clear All** in the table header to reset cooldowns for all tracks simultaneously.
