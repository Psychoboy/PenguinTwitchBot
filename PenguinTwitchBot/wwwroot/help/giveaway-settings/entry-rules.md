# Giveaway Entry Rules & Costs

Control how viewers enter giveaways, ticket costs, and currency integration.

## Entry Parameters

- **Point Type Selector**: Choose which currency (from **Point Settings**) is required to purchase giveaway tickets (e.g. Points, Giveaway Tickets, or Event Tokens).
- **Cost Per Entry**: The amount of points deducted per ticket entry.
- **Maximum Entries Per User**: Cap total tickets any individual viewer can buy to maintain fair odds across the audience.
- **Subscriber Multipliers**: Optional bonus ticket multipliers awarded to channel subscribers (Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3).
- **Eligibility Filters**: Exclude users with fewer than a minimum watch-time threshold or active chatter requirements.

## Chat Commands for Entries

- `!enter <amount>`: Viewers spend points to purchase tickets into the current giveaway.
- `!entries`: Viewers check their own entry count and odds in chat.
- `!setprize <prize>`: Broadcasters quickly update the current prize name from chat.
